Lynden Lager

Lynden Lager


ABV: 4.5%
IBU’S: 20


Tasting Notes

Light Gold, Highly Drinkable, Floral, Light Body

Description

Brewed with our old friends at Overflow Taps! A beautiful little lager made specially for their taprooms. Light crisp, and meant to be enjoyed in quantity at any one of their two wonderful taprooms and beyond!

Food Pairing

Enjoy Lynden Lager with a classic wood-fire pizza.
